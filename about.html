About NextSeason

Great shows stay with you.
The dates don’t always.

NextSeason gives your favorite shows a place to live between seasons.

The idea is simple

Pick up where
anticipation left off.

You finish a season. You find something else to watch. Months go by, and the show you meant to come back to slips your mind.

NextSeason is built around that everyday gap: a simple watchlist, a place to check what’s next, and one less thing to remember.
