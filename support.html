Common questions

What does NextSeason do?

NextSeason helps you keep track of the shows you’re watching and notifies you when new seasons are announced, premiere dates become available, or other important information changes.

How many shows can I track for free?

You can keep up to three shows on your watchlist for free. NextSeason Plus removes that limit. Learn about Plus.

Premiere dates and notifications

NextSeason uses third-party TV listings. Premiere dates can be missing, inaccurate, or change after they’re announced. Dates generally reflect the original broadcast and may differ from when a season is available in your country or on your streaming service.

Alerts depend on when updated listings become available, when iOS lets NextSeason check in the background, and your notification settings. An alert may arrive late or may not appear. Opening NextSeason occasionally helps keep your watchlist up to date.

Why doesn’t my show have a premiere date?

A new season may not have an announced date yet, or the date may change after it is announced.

Why does NextSeason say a season is airing when I can't watch it yet?

NextSeason uses the show's original broadcast schedule. A season may begin airing in one country before it becomes available elsewhere or on streaming services. NextSeason shows the original broadcaster when that information is available, but it doesn't currently track availability by country or streaming service.

Where can I find Plus pricing?

Learn about Plus explains everything about Plus, including pricing.

How do I report incorrect show information?

Email support@getnextseason.com with the show’s name and what looks wrong. If you have a link to the broadcaster’s announcement or schedule, include it. If you’ve confirmed the error is in the source listing, you can request a correction through TheTVDB Support or TVmaze’s Edit Requests forum.

How do I report a problem with NextSeason?

Email support@getnextseason.com. Include the show’s name or a description of what happened. For an app issue, your device model, app version, and a screenshot can help us understand the problem. Please leave out passwords and payment details.
