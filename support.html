Common questions

What does NextSeason do?

NextSeason helps you keep track of the shows you’re watching and notifies you when new seasons are announced, premiere dates become available, or other important information changes.

How many shows can I track for free?

You can keep up to three shows on your watchlist for free. NextSeason Plus removes that limit. Learn about Plus.

Why doesn’t my show have a premiere date?

A new season may not have an announced date yet, or the date may change after it is announced. You will receive notifications as release information becomes available.

Where can I find Plus pricing?

Learn about Plus explains everything about Plus, including pricing.

How do I report incorrect show information?

NextSeason gets its show information from TheTVDB. If the information is also incorrect there, corrections should be reported to TheTVDB's Support Center. If TheTVDB has the correct information but NextSeason doesn’t, see "How do I report a problem with NextSeason?" below.

How do I report a problem with NextSeason?

Email support@getnextseason.com. Include the show’s name or a description of what happened. For an app issue, your device model, app version, and a screenshot can help us understand the problem. Please leave out passwords and payment details.
